# User Guide

## Signing in
You will receive an activation link from your administrator. Open it, choose a password (at least
10 characters with upper- and lowercase letters and a number) and sign in with your work email.
After repeated failed attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes.

## Raising a purchase request
1. Click **New request**.
2. **Request information** — your name, job title, department and the date are filled from your
   profile. Choose the purchase **category** (this decides who approves first) and write a short summary.
3. **Business justification** — explain the purpose and need. Vendor bank/payment details go in the
   *restricted* box: only you, your approvers and authorised finance staff can see them.
4. **Purchase items** — add one line per item: description, quantity, currency and VAT-inclusive
   unit price. Totals calculate automatically (the server re-checks them). Imperial EdTech requests
   use one currency per request (NGN by default).
5. **Supporting documents** — attach quotations, invoices or specifications (PDF, images, Word,
   Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, text; up to 10 MB each).
6. **Review & submit** — tick the confirmation and submit. You will see your PR number
   (e.g. `IET-PR-2026-0012`) and the next approver.

Use **Save draft** at any time; drafts are visible only to you (My Requests). The browser warns you
before leaving with unsaved changes.

## After submission
Your request moves through: Pending Level 1 → Pending Finance → Pending Final → **Approved**.
You are notified at each step. Open the request to see the approval timeline and comments.

- **Returned for clarification**: read the approver's comment, click *Update & resubmit*, change
  what is needed and resubmit. The earlier version is kept in the history.
- **Rejected**: the reason is shown on the request. Rejected requests stay in your history.
- **Cancel**: you can cancel a request that is not yet approved or rejected (a reason is required).
- **PDF**: download the PR document at any time after submission; once fully approved it shows all
  three approval records.

## For approvers
**Approvals** lists the requests waiting for you, oldest first (overdue items are flagged). Open a
request, review items, attachments and history, then choose:
- **Approve** — optional comment; the request moves to the next level automatically.
- **Clarify** — required comment; the request returns to the requestor.
- **Reject** — required reason; the request is closed.

You cannot approve a request you raised yourself, and you can only act when the request is at your
stage. Your decision is recorded with your name, role, date and time; it cannot be changed afterwards.
These are internal approval records, not certified electronic signatures.

## Notifications
The bell shows unread notifications. Click one to open the request; use *Mark all as read* to clear
them. If email is enabled you also receive an email for each notification.
